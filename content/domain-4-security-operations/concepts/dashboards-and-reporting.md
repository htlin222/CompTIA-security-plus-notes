## Definition

Dashboards and reporting in SIEM platforms provide visual representations of security data, enabling analysts and security managers to quickly understand the current security posture, identify trends, and communicate status to stakeholders. Dashboards offer real-time or near-real-time views, while reports compile historical data for compliance, management briefings, and trend analysis.

## Key Details

- Operational dashboards show real-time alert queues, active incidents, and current threat levels
- Executive dashboards show high-level KPIs and security posture summaries for non-technical audiences
- Compliance reports demonstrate adherence to regulatory requirements (PCI DSS, HIPAA, SOX)
- Trend reports identify increasing or decreasing threat categories over time
- Custom dashboards can be built for specific roles: SOC analyst, CISO, compliance officer
